HERE’S THE HANDY PART

Your hands are the controls.

  1. Make two pinches. Touch your thumb and index finger on each hand. Keep both hands in the camera view.
  2. Frame a moment. Move your pinches apart diagonally. The glowing square shows the exact area that will become your puzzle.
  3. Release to capture. Open either pinch. Your camera photo freezes inside the square and shuffles into nine pieces.
  4. Put it back together. Pinch over a tile, carry it over another, and release to swap. Correct pieces glow and stay in place.

Keep the room bright and your hands about an arm’s length from the camera. You can also choose mouse / touch controls.
