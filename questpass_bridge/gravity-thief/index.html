A LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

Get a feel for gravity.

Camera: keep one hand visible. Close your hand into a fist to pull. Move your fist to steer; open your hand to release.

Mouse / touch: hold anywhere in the play area to pull toward that spot. Release to coast.

Keyboard: arrow keys move the field. Hold Space to pull. Esc pauses; R restarts.

To brake, hold the field near the core. To turn, guide it in small steps. Walls and screen edges give a gentle bump so you can recover. A dark, dashed laser is safe to cross. Every level can be retried.

Bright, even light helps tracking. A larger play area or landscape view gives you more room. Camera mode pauses when your hand leaves view.
